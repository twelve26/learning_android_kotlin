# A07.3 — Cancellation and retry policy

## Prerequisites
Complete the previous Android level and its gate. Finish the previous stage in this project.

## Concept in context
WorkManager schedules deferrable persistent work. Enqueueing is not immediate execution. Unique work prevents duplicates; observe WorkInfo rather than assuming a click means completion.

## Build this
Add cancellation. Extend the worker with an injected failing writer and bounded retry policy.
